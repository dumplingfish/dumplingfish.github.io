How to delete your Mahjong Mini account (made by dumplingfish) and the data that goes with it. You can do it in the game, or ask us by email if you no longer have the game installed.

In the game

  1. Open Mahjong Mini and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Profile, then Account.
  3. Tap Delete Account, then Delete for Good.

The account is deleted straight away.

By email

Write to us from the email address you signed in with, or tell us the sign-in method you used (Google, Apple, Discord or email) and your display name, so we can find the account. We delete it within 30 days and reply when it is done.

Request deletion support@dumplingfish.com

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in and account.
  • Your profile and progress: display name, coins, Jade, items, levels, missions, friends, match history and replays.

What is kept

  • Purchase records, for as long as needed for refunds, fraud prevention and tax and accounting law.
  • Ban records, for as long as a ban lasts and a reasonable time after.
  • Anonymous hand records, which contain nothing that links them to you or your account.
  • Server logs, for a few days.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Club subscription: it is billed by your app store, so cancel it in Google Play or App Store settings. If you play as a guest (without signing in), your progress is tied to a random ID on your device rather than an account; email us and we will help remove it. More in our Privacy Policy.
