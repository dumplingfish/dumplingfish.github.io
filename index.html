Mahjong Mini

Real mahjong, quick and cosy

The classic table game with every rule intact, big clear tiles, and rounds that fit in a coffee break. New to mahjong? Bao, your guide, teaches you one idea at a time.

Play in your browser
  • Hong Kong, Sichuan, Singaporean and Taiwanese rules
  • Solo against bots, with friends at a private table, or online
  • Three daily puzzles, missions and weekly cups
  • Played for points only: nothing is staked

Mahjong School

Never played?
Bao will teach you!

Lessons cover one idea at a time: the three suits, pong, chow and kong, building a hand, and when to let a tile go.

At the table, Bao coaches you, and hints show which tiles would win when you are one away.

Play together

Mahjong night.
Anywhere.

Open a private table, share the code and pick the rules. Short a player? A bot takes the empty seat, so nobody waits long.

Or jump into an online quick match, casual or ranked.

Every day

Your daily
mahjong fix.

Three new puzzles a day train you to read the table, like finding the discard that keeps the most tiles working for you.

Missions, a login calendar and weekly cups keep every visit rewarding.

Four rule sets

Can you beat
Auntie Lan?

Bots with their own personalities and pace, from relaxed to sharp. Hong Kong, Sichuan, Singaporean and Taiwanese rules, each with its own scoring and house rules you can adjust.

Rules sheets explain the differences in plain words.
